#4471 holds every report about dates that came in from April to 4 October. The desk merged them by subject line (decision 0001) and marked the ticket known: one bug, one fix queued. Read by cause instead of by subject, the same reports hold three problems. One fix closes only one of them.

Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October: the parser is fixed and the hint won't be fixed, because the booking-v3 form is retired. Engineering's update does not mention the one-day move, so its ticket stays open.

The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Tenants whose locale writes the day first get the month and day swapped: a booking for 7 April goes out on 4 July. This is the bug #4471 names, and the queued fix is for it.

hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Tenants on booking-v3 in day-first locales (Alder Physio, Copper Studio, Pale Harbour) get people who type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. Only reports that came through the form have this cause. The parser fix does not touch it: the fix is the hint (or reading the field the way the hint asks).

one-day: the date moves by one day, not by a swapped month. “Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” The desk already read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) and still filed them under parser by tenant, where they are still counted. No month flips, so neither the parser fix nor the hint fix touches them. It looks like a time zone or midnight conversion bug.

Outcome

From engineering’s update of 5 October. This page has not checked the fix against new reports: the export ends on 4 October.

  • parser: fixed, fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. Ticket closed as completed.
  • hint: won’t fix, the booking-v3 form is retired. Ticket closed as not planned.
  • one-day: not in the update. Nobody has fixed it or decided not to, so its ticket stays open. It is a different mechanism (time zone or midnight conversion), so the parser fix may not cover it.

How these were counted

  • The export comes in pages that overlap, so some reports appear twice. A report counts once, by id.
  • The cause comes from the tenant: its locale and its booking form (causes.ts). The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser and is not used.
  • Reports the desk read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) have a ticket of their own, and stay counted under the cause the desk files them by, with a footnote: the desk’s own view.

Lessons

  • Merging by subject line counts reports, not causes. A known ticket can hold bugs nobody is fixing.
  • Count from the data at render time, so the page stays right when the queue moves.
